# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-15

Refactor of the original Colab notebook into an installable, tested package, and revised edition of the report.

### Added
- `panelfoil` package (`src/panelfoil/`): geometry, solver, studies (mesh convergence, inverse design), XFOIL readers and plotting.
- `panelfoil` command that reproduces every table and figure (`--json` writes the numbers).
- `pf.system()`: the (N+1)×(N+1) system as written out by hand, used to reproduce the three-panel wedge.
- Lift from pressure integration (`Solution.cl_pressure`) as a cross-check of the circulation lift.
- Test suite: report values, equivalence with the original element-by-element solver, physics checks (zero lift and moment for a symmetric section, nose-down moment for positive camber, thin-airfoil lift slope, Kutta condition, outward normals), comparison with XFOIL.
- XFOIL outputs shipped in `src/panelfoil/data/` (no Google Drive dependency).
- CI: ruff, pytest on Python 3.9–3.13, command-line reproduction and notebook execution.

### Changed
- Influence coefficients vectorized with NumPy: identical to the original to 1e-12; the full design search takes about half a second.
- Notebook rewritten as `notebooks/panel_method_analysis.ipynb`, using the package.

### Fixed
- Pitching moment sign: C_m,c/4 is now positive nose-up, so cambered sections give negative values, consistent with XFOIL.
- Hand calculation of the wedge: consistent clockwise contour (panel 1 normal pointing out of the body) and free-stream terms in the Kutta equation. γ = 0.211, C_l = 0.873.
- Report translated into English, with the figures regenerated by the package.
- Report: equations 9–12, 15 and 17; maximum-camber position of the NACA 3217 (20 %); camber increase of the redesign (one point); airfoil of the viscous study (NACA 4114); viscous C_l,max angle (17°); caveat on the non-monotonic mesh convergence; empty equation numbers; figure reference in section 2.5.
